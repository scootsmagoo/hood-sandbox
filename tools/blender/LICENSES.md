# Licences of the Blender assets

Everything under `public/assets/` that `npm run assets` writes is **generated by the scripts in
`tools/blender/`** (MIT, like the rest of the repo). No third-party model, texture, brush or
photograph is used or embedded: trees, lamps, hydrants, bins, bollards, cars and **people** are built from
numbers and a seeded random generator, and leaf shapes and faces are procedural (computed in the shader).
Blender's output carries no licence obligation.

Deliberately not used: the Quaternius tree, nature and character packs (Quaternius Asset License v1.0,
which is not CC0 and forbids redistributing the asset files, modified or not; this repo is public),
and anything from Mixamo (its characters and animations may not be redistributed as files in a repo).

**Characters** (`public/assets/characters/characters.glb`, `tools/blender/characters/`): the bodies
(sixteen archetypes, three levels of detail), the skeleton, the skin weights and all thirty-one
animation clips are written in Python from numbers in the repo: proportions from the sim's ragdoll
(`src/shared/ragdoll.ts`) and ordinary anthropometric figures (a 1.74 m adult, hip joint at 0.91 m,
and so on), gaits from a two-bone leg solver, poses from a two-bone arm solver aimed at hand
targets. There is no scan, no sculpt, no motion capture, no MakeHuman/MPFB2 base mesh and no
texture: skin, hair, brows, eyes and lips are drawn by the ped shader (`src/client/render/chars/pedMaterial.ts`).
The CMU motion-capture database (free to use, but its terms ask for credit and it is not a clearly
licensed redistributable asset set) was looked at as a source for the walk and run and not used.

**Hero landmarks** (`public/assets/landmarks/*.glb`, `tools/blender/landmarks/`): original, script-built interpretations of
real Downtown Atlanta buildings, drawn from public descriptions, measurements and photographs consulted for reference only
(shapes, proportions, colours, heights and footprints from OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps data; no photograph, texture,
drawing or model is copied or embedded). They carry **no brand marks, logos, wordmarks or signage**: the real buildings'
signs (the stadium's star, the arena's and the museum's lettering, the hotels' and the banks' names) are left off, and the game
shows parody names for trademarked brands (`displayName` in `data/cities/<city>/landmarks.json`). The Olympic rings'
colours and marks are not used on the Fountain of Rings (monochrome jets in the same arrangement). Architecture is stylised
in the way the rest of the game is, not traced. **A legal review of the landmark models is recommended before any commercial
release** (trade dress and architectural-work rights vary; the README already says so).
